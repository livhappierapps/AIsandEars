# Research findings

## Preliminary findings (source discovery)

- Search results surfaced peer-reviewed studies of AI-companion loss, including a 2024 study of users affected by Soulmate's shutdown; the evidence is about human reports of loss, not proof that the AI experienced death or loss. [Evidence: `research/sources/attachment-01-companion-loss.md`; search result for PMID 42857905 also surfaced a 2026 paper but needs verification.]
- Indexed forum/community search results are useful for finding feature wishes and complaints, but they are self-selected anecdotes and should not be treated as representative demand.
- Replit's current spend guidance describes usage monitoring, Agent modes, limits/budgets, and Plan Mode as cost controls. [Evidence: `research/sources/replit-02-managing-spend.md`]
- Replit's TestFlight guide says the uploaded build is managed in App Store Connect; internal testing and external beta review have different steps. [Evidence: `research/sources/replit-03-testflight.md`]
- Apple's official App Store Connect API page lists automation for app metadata, builds/TestFlight, submissions, provisioning, reviews, and reporting; credentials and permission scope need further checking. [Evidence: `research/sources/apple-01-app-store-connect-api.md`]
- Replit documentation search summaries gave inconsistent statements about Android publishing. The Expo project guidance says Replit Expo Launch handles iOS/App Store submission only and Google Play publishing is not supported in that flow; report that distinction and cite the official product docs after verification.
- An academic paper abstract page on loneliness was fetchable but did not expose its findings; do not cite it for effect claims until a primary abstract/full text is found.
