# Privacy Policy — AIs&Ears

**Effective date:** June 18, 2026
**Last updated:** June 18, 2026

AIs&Ears is operated by Liv Happier Apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

---

## 1. What We Collect

### Information you provide
- **Payment information** — processed exclusively by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We retain only the Stripe session ID and the tier you purchased.
- **Email address** — only if you contact us directly or if a future account system is introduced.

### Information collected automatically during a session
- **Session metadata** — session ID, tier (audio or full), session start time, and expiration time. Stored in our database to authenticate your session.
- **Audio** — your voice is streamed in real time to xAI's servers for AI processing. **We do not record, store, or transcribe your audio.** Audio exists only in-transit and is discarded once the session ends.
- **Camera frames** — if you enable Camera Vision, still frames (one every ~3 seconds) are sent to xAI's servers for visual processing. **We do not store these images.** They are transmitted and discarded.
- **Screen frames** — if you enable Screen Sharing, still frames of your screen are sent to xAI's servers. **We do not store these frames.**

### Information we do NOT collect
- We do not store transcripts of your conversations.
- We do not retain audio recordings.
- We do not retain images or screen captures.
- We do not build behavioral profiles.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Stripe session ID + tier | Authenticate paid session access |
| Audio (in transit) | Power Leo's real-time voice responses via xAI |
| Camera / screen frames (in transit) | Power Leo's real-time visual responses via xAI |
| Session expiry | Enforce 30-minute session windows |

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

### Stripe
Payments are processed by [Stripe, Inc.](https://stripe.com). When you purchase a session or subscription, you interact directly with Stripe's checkout. Your payment data is governed by [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

### xAI
The AI (Leo) is powered by [xAI](https://x.ai). Your real-time audio, camera frames, and screen frames are transmitted to xAI's API. xAI's data handling is governed by [xAI's Privacy Policy](https://x.ai/legal/privacy-policy).

---

## 4. Data Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Paid session records | 90 days after session expiry, then deleted |
| Audio / video / screen streams | Never stored — real-time transit only |
| Stripe payment records | Retained by Stripe per their policy |

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

AIs&Ears is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the service, please contact us and we will promptly delete any associated records.

---

## 6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures: encrypted transport (TLS/WSS), server-side-only AI keys, CORS restrictions, and payment session validation. No audio or video is written to disk at any point.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you (limited to session records)
- Request deletion of your session records
- Object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@livhappierapps.com**.
