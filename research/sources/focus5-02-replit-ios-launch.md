Uploading puts your app inside App Store Connect. Nothing before this point is visible to
Apple, and nothing after it, TestFlight or submitting for review, works until it’s done.You’ll need an [Apple Developer account](https://docs.replit.com/build/mobile-apple-account) first.

## [​](https://docs.replit.com/build/mobile-upload-ios\#what-gets-uploaded)  What gets uploaded

A **build** is a real, installable copy of your app, the same kind of file you’d get from the
App Store, except it isn’t published yet. Expo Go runs your code inside a shared preview app,
a build is your app on its own, which is why features like push notifications and in-app
purchases only work here.Replit’s **Launch** feature creates that build, signs it, and uploads it from the
[Publishing](https://docs.replit.com/features/publishing/overview) tab.

1

Publish the web app first

PixelGames’ backend needs to be live before the App Store launch, select **Publish** at
the top of your workspace to publish it.

![Replit editor with the Publish button highlighted](https://mintcdn.com/replit/wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6/images/pixelgames/mobile/publish-step0.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6&q=85&s=8649996b14285d251725e88fa510cf48)

Publishing PixelGames' web app and API server first.

2

Start publishing to the App Store

Once the web app is published, a **Start publishing to the App Store** button appears
under **Publishing**. Select it to open the Launch wizard.

![Replit Publishing tab with the Start publishing to the App Store button](https://mintcdn.com/replit/wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6/images/pixelgames/mobile/publish-step1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6&q=85&s=9f7741ffeee02d92322bc8760a53b322)

Starting the App Store launch from Replit's Publishing tab.

3

Choose your Expo project

Confirm the project name and slug, or select **Use existing** if PixelGames already has
one.

![Launch wizard Choose Expo project step with project name and slug fields](https://mintcdn.com/replit/wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6/images/pixelgames/mobile/publish-step2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6&q=85&s=12091ccccb5b62910f941d8435fde3bb)

Choosing the Expo project to launch.

4

Sign in with Apple

Use the same Apple account enrolled in the Apple Developer Program. Replit uses these
credentials only to authenticate with Apple and does not store your password.

![Launch wizard Sign in with Apple step with email and password fields](https://mintcdn.com/replit/wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6/images/pixelgames/mobile/publish-step4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6&q=85&s=5fdf6a70851c6b2d8dc92b8580952b63)

Signing in with Apple to connect the Expo project.

5

Choose your Apple app

Confirm the app name and bundle ID, or select **Use existing** if you already created one
in App Store Connect.

**The mobile app bundle ID is permanent once you’ve published**The bundle ID used for your first publish is tied to your Replit project. Changing it
afterward causes the next App Store publish to fail.To publish under a different bundle ID, remix the project or create a new one. The new
App Store Connect app starts fresh, without the existing listing, TestFlight builds, or
reviews.

![Launch wizard Choose Apple app step with app name and bundle identifier fields](https://mintcdn.com/replit/wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6/images/pixelgames/mobile/publish-step5.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6&q=85&s=12d9fc538428a2bc11688fbf26aa93b9)

Confirming the app name and bundle ID.

6

Configure Apple certificates

Launch selects a valid distribution certificate and provisioning profile automatically,
or creates new ones if none exist yet.

![Launch wizard Configure Apple certificates step](https://mintcdn.com/replit/wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6/images/pixelgames/mobile/publish-step6.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6&q=85&s=c239bed35a8bc200631bf7d93f07e157)

Launch configuring Apple's distribution certificate and provisioning profile.

7

Launch to the App Store

Once every step above is checked off, select **Launch to App Store**. Your build compiles
on Expo’s servers, then lands in App Store Connect.

![Launch wizard Ready for launch step with the Launch to App Store button](https://mintcdn.com/replit/wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6/images/pixelgames/mobile/publish-step7.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6&q=85&s=5731fe06230c3945b25a28c974b50195)

All steps complete, ready to launch to the App Store.

8

Wait for publishing to finish

Publishing can take up to 45 minutes. You can keep building with Agent while it runs, and
installing the Replit app gets you a notification when it completes.

![Replit Publishing tab showing Publishing to App Store in progress](https://mintcdn.com/replit/wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6/images/pixelgames/mobile/publish-step9.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wpDFPJulnZRpqmW6&q=85&s=97e26725b6a51cd6ad8e367c339337d2)

Publishing to the App Store in progress, tracked from the Publishing tab.

9

Open it in TestFlight

When publishing completes you get an email titled **“\[Your name\] has invited you to test**
**PixelGames”**. Select **View in TestFlight** to open the app and install it on your phone.

Once it finishes, your build appears in App Store Connect under your app.From there it can go two ways: hand it to
[testers on TestFlight](https://docs.replit.com/build/mobile-testflight) first, or go straight to the listing and
[submit it for review](https://docs.replit.com/build/mobile-publish-ios). Nothing is public yet either way, the
build just sits in App Store Connect until you submit it.

## [​](https://docs.replit.com/build/mobile-upload-ios\#uploading-again-later)  Uploading again later

Changed something in PixelGames? Run the same flow again to get the new version up.It goes much faster the second time. Your Expo project, Apple account, app, and certificates
are all still filled in, so most steps are just confirming what’s already there.

## [​](https://docs.replit.com/build/mobile-upload-ios\#troubleshooting)  Troubleshooting

- **No distribution certificates found:** normal on a first launch, select **Create new**
**certificate** and Launch generates one.
- **Sign in with Apple fails:** confirm the account is enrolled in the Apple Developer
Program, not just a regular Apple ID.
- **Publishing seems stuck:** it can take up to 45 minutes, and builds are queued at peak
times. Check your Expo dashboard under **Builds** before assuming something’s broken.
