# The app that describes the world for you

Be My Eyes provides the world’s largest online community of people who are blind or have low vision with seamless access to real-time visual assistance. Through our app, users can connect with a global network of volunteers, specialist company representatives, and visual AI to receive live descriptions, product support, and guidance—all for free.

A man wearing a blue jacket, white shirt and headphones is sitting outdoors holding a white cane. A tree and building are behind him. Generated with Be My AI.

Features

Helping hands, smart technology, and visual description wherever you are

![phone icon](https://www.bemyeyes.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/6.svg)

Call a volunteer

The app connects users with volunteers instantly. They’ll use your phone’s outward-facing camera to provide real-time descriptions and assist with everyday tasks.

![stars icon](https://www.bemyeyes.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/12.svg)

Be My AI

Users can leverage artificial intelligence (AI) to receive vivid image and scene descriptions, along with in-depth, context-specific help, with no need to connect to a volunteer.

![book icon](https://www.bemyeyes.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/13.svg)

Service Directory

The app provides a convenient central directory of accessible service and help desks of leading organizations from around the world – making support just a tap away.

![globe icon](https://www.bemyeyes.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/9.svg)

Worldwide

The app is available for free across 150 countries and 180 languages, with over 750,000 users and more than 8 million volunteers already connected.

## Find the support you need, when you need it

Our Service Directory is a free, centralized place to find accessible service centers from leading brands, businesses, and government departments – all on our Be My Eyes app.

Companies listed in the directory provide key details like contact numbers, services, and country-specific information to make it easy to connect with the support you need.

Some organizations also offer enhanced listings with more details, multiple brand options, and support in different languages to help you access the right assistance, wherever you are.

[Browse Service Directory](https://directory.bemyeyes.com/en-US/service-directory/gb)
