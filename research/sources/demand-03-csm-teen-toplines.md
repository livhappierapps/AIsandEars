# Talk, Trust, and Trade-offs: How and Why Teens Use AI Companions

# Toplines*

**This report examines how U.S. teens age 13 to 17 currently use AI companions, drawing from a** **nationally representative survey of 1,060 teens conducted in April and May 2025.**

## Q1. How often do you interact with AI companions?

Base: All respondents (n=1,060)

- Several times a day: 8%
- Once a day: 5%
- A few times a week: 21%
- Once a week: 9%
- A few times a month: 9%
- I have tried them but rarely use them: 19%
- I have never used an AI companion: 28% ***Ever used: 72%*** ***Regular users (a few times a month or more): 52%***
## Q2. Which of the following describes how you use or view AI companions? (Select all that apply)

Base: All respondents (n=1,060)

- For conversation or social practice: 18%
- For emotional or mental health support: 12%
- For romantic or flirtatious interactions: 8%
- For role-playing or imaginative scenarios: 12%
- As a friend or best friend: 9%
***● Social interaction (respondents who selected at least one of the above): 33%***
- As a tool or program: 46%
- None of these: 33%
- Other (please specify): 4%

## Q3. Why do you use AI companions? (Select up to 3)

Base: AI companion users only (n=758)

- It's entertaining: 30%
- I'm curious about the technology: 28%
- It gives advice: 18%
- They're always available when I need someone to talk to: 17%
- They don't judge me: 14%
- I can say things I wouldn't tell my friends or family: 12%
- It's easier than talking to real people: 9%
- It helps me practice social skills: 7%
- It helps me feel less lonely: 6%
- Other: 5%
## Q4. How much do you trust the information or advice from AI companions?

Base: All respondents (n=1,060)

- Not at all: 24%
- A little: 26%
- Somewhat: 27%
- Quite a bit: 20%
- Completely: 3%
***● Do Not Trust (Not at all + A little): 50%***
***● Trust (Quite a bit + Completely): 23%***
## Q5. How do conversations with AI companions compare to conversations with your real-life friends? Are they…

### Base: All respondents (n=1,060)

- Much less satisfying: 47%
- Somewhat less satisfying: 20%
- About the same: 21%
- Somewhat more satisfying: 7%
- Much more satisfying: 3%
***● More Satisfying (Somewhat more satisfying + Much more satisfying): 10%***
***● Less Satisfying (Somewhat less satisfying + Much less satisfying): 67%***

## Q6. Have you used any of the following skills you've practiced with AI companions in real-life

## situations? (Select all that apply)

- Conversation starters: 18%
- Expressing emotions: 13%
- Standing up for myself: 11%
- Apologizing or resolving conflicts: 11%
- Romantic interactions: 8%
- Giving advice: 14%
- ***Social interaction (respondents who selected at least one of the above): 33%***
- I don't use AI companions to practice social skills: 60%
- Other (please specify): 1%
## Q7. How often do you interact with AI companions compared to spending time with friends (online or

## in-person)? Do you spend…

Base: AI companion users only (n=758)

- Much more time with friends: 68%
- Somewhat more time with friends: 12%
- About the same amount of time with friends and AI companions: 13%
- Somewhat more time with AI companions: 5%
- Much more time with AI companions: 1%
***● More Time With Friends (NET): 80% ● More Time With AI Companions (NET): 6%***
## Q8. Have you ever felt uncomfortable with something an AI companion has said or done?

Base: AI companion users only (n=758)

***● Yes (NET): 34%***
- Yes, frequently: 6%
- Yes, occasionally: 9%
- Yes, once or twice: 19%
- No, never: 66%
## Q9. Have you ever chosen to talk to an AI companion instead of a real person about something

## important or serious?

Base: AI companion users only (n=758)

***● Yes (NET): 33%***
- Yes, frequently: 4%
- Yes, occasionally: 12%
- Yes, once or twice: 17%
- No, never: 66%

## Q10. Have you ever shared personal or private information (e.g., real name, location, secrets) with an AI companion?

***● Yes (NET): 24%***
- Yes, frequently: 4%
- Yes, occasionally: 8%
- Yes, once or twice: 13%
- No, never: 74%
**Note: May not sum to 100% because of rounding or nonresponse.*
