These answers cover plans and pricing. For full plan details, see [Starter](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/starter-plan), [Core](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/replit-core), and [Pro](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/replit-pro), and check the [pricing page](https://replit.com/pricing) for current rates.

How is Agent usage priced, and why am I charged in Plan mode?

Free Mode provides no-cost Agent work within each plan’s allowance. The Core and Pro allowances reset every five hours and have weekly limits. The Starter tier keeps its existing limits. Check [Usage](https://replit.com/settings/usage) in **Settings** to see your usage.On Core and Pro, select Auto in Power or Max to let Replit choose a model for paid work. You can also choose an available model manually.Paid modes and confirmed paid work use [effort-based pricing](https://docs.replit.com/billing/ai-billing) based on request complexity. Plan Mode can perform billable reasoning in the paid path, even when it does not change code. Replit asks for confirmation before a paid action starts.Start with [Free Mode](https://docs.replit.com/features/agent/agent-modes) for everyday work. Choose [Power mode](https://docs.replit.com/features/agent/agent-modes) when you need more capability at an affordable price. You can also scope work with [Plan mode](https://docs.replit.com/features/agent/plan-mode), and set a usage limit and budget in [Managing your spend](https://docs.replit.com/billing/managing-spend). To see a breakdown of your charges, go to [Account usage](https://replit.com/settings/usage) in **Settings** and select **See previous invoices**.

What's included in each plan—Starter, Core, and Pro?

Each plan targets a different stage of building:

- **Starter** is free, with a daily Agent allowance and limited monthly cloud credits, basic Agent access, and one published app. See [Starter plan](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/starter-plan).
- **Core** includes $20 in monthly credits, full Agent with Plan and Build modes, 1 parallel Agent task, up to 5 collaborators, unlimited published apps, and a 7-day database restore window. See [Core plan](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/replit-core).
- **Pro** ($100/month) adds more Free Mode usage, up to 10 parallel Agent tasks, and up to 15 builders. It also includes tiered credits and a 28-day database restore window. Unused credits roll over for two months. See [Pro plan](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/replit-pro).

For a side-by-side comparison and current pricing, see the [pricing page](https://replit.com/pricing).

Does Replit offer a student or education discount?

Replit runs an education program for students and educators. To check current eligibility, discounts, and how to apply, visit [replit.com/education](https://replit.com/education).

Specific discount amounts and eligibility rules are managed by the education program and may change—the education page is the source of truth.

How does annual billing work, and how much do I save?

Annual billing gives you a lower effective monthly rate in exchange for paying for the year upfront. [Core](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/replit-core) is $204/year. To switch, choose the annual option when you subscribe or change your plan. For other plans and current savings, see the [pricing page](https://replit.com/pricing).

Do unused monthly credits roll over to the next month?

It depends on your plan. Core credits reset at the start of each billing cycle and don’t roll over. Pro includes [credit rollover](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/replit-pro)—unused credits carry over for two months before expiring.If your balance is higher than your monthly allocation, the extra may be referral or promotional credits, which have their own expiration dates.

How do I purchase additional credits (credit packs)?

Credit packs add credits on top of your monthly allocation, with discounts on larger packs:

| Credit Pack | Price | Discount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $100 credits | $100 | — |
| $300 credits | $290 | $10 off |
| $500 credits | $480 | $20 off |
| $1,000 credits | $950 | $50 off |

Go to [Billing](https://replit.com/settings/billing) in **Settings** and select the option to purchase a credit pack. Credits apply to Agent usage, deployments, storage, and other usage-based services. See [Managing your spend](https://docs.replit.com/billing/managing-spend#purchase-credit-packs) for details.If you subscribed through the App Store, add a web payment method to your account first.

Is there a free trial for Core or Pro?

Replit doesn’t offer a time-limited free trial for paid plans, but you have options to try Replit first:

- The [Starter plan](https://docs.replit.com/billing/plans/starter-plan) is free with no time limit.
- The [education program](https://replit.com/education) offers discounted access to eligible students.

For current subscription refund terms, [contact Replit Support](https://replit.com/support).

If I upgrade mid-cycle, how is proration calculated?

When you change plans mid-cycle, Replit credits the unused portion of your current plan toward the cost of the new plan. The exact proration is shown on the billing confirmation screen before you complete the change, so you can review it before anything is charged.
